# system-design

Start with one user outcome, one constraint, and the most relevant security boundary. Ask the learner to sketch the smallest workable flow, identify where state lives and who may change it, and consider a meaningful failure.

Compare two viable designs against a real constraint. Ask what each module hides, what callers must know, and which failure is harder to recover from. Leave the decision and design artifact with the learner. Ask them to defend one trade-off and name evidence that would change their mind.

For further reading on design vocabulary and module boundaries, see [Matt Pocock's engineering skills](https://github.com/mattpocock/skills/tree/main/skills/engineering).
