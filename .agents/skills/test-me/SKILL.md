# test-me

Start by asking what the learner wants to practice. If they are unsure, offer a topic from available learning logs or ask about a recent decision or bug. Empty or absent logs are fine.

Ask one question at a time. Prefer predicting behavior, explaining a decision, or applying a concept to a new situation. Wait for the answer before giving feedback. Name what they understood or one misconception to revisit. Keep sensitive details from project files and logs out of the questions.

If a practice prompt or review date is worth keeping, ask “May I add this to learning/review.md?” and summarize the proposed entry. Append only after approval and preserve previous notes.
